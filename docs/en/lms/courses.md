# Courses

**Role:** Teacher, content author, or workspace owner.

**Goal:** Build a course outline from existing content and review how learners will open it.

![Course builder with Sections, Course items, Player, and Reports tabs](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses.png)

## What You Need

-   Create or find the page and link resources that belong in the course.
-   Open Courses from the sidebar or create a course from a content project.
-   Confirm that you can edit course outline records in the workspace.

## Workflow

1. Open Courses, select Compliance Refresh Course, and confirm its certificate-policy section appears.
   ![Courses step 1](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses-step-1.png)
2. Open Course items to review the items attached to the selected course.
   ![Courses step 2](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses-step-2.png)
3. Use Create in Course items to open the item form. Choose related content by its visible title, and cancel if you are only reviewing the available fields.
   ![Courses step 3](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses-step-3.png)
4. In Sections, open a section's action menu to review its options without changing the course.
   ![Courses step 4](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses-step-4.png)
5. Open Reports to review the selected course's activity.
   ![Courses step 5](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/courses-step-5.png)

## Screen Details

| Area               | How to use it                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Course selection   | The selected course scopes the related Sections and Course items shown in the builder. Use its visible title to confirm the context.   |
| Sections           | Review section order and use the available actions to maintain the course structure.                                                   |
| Course items       | Review or create the items attached to the selected course. Relation controls should show readable titles rather than raw identifiers. |
| Player and Reports | Use Player to inspect the learner experience and Reports to review available course activity.                                          |

## Result

Course sections and items are stored as workspace content. The builder exposes the selected course's related records, the learner Player, and available Reports in separate tabs.

## What To Check

Course builders should keep toolbar controls aligned and should not create page-level horizontal scrolling.
