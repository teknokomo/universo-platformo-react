# Learning Tracks

**Role:** Teacher, content author, or workspace owner.

**Goal:** Create an ordered learning path with stages, steps, and learner progress.

![Learning track builder with stages and steps](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks.png)

## What You Need

-   Prepare the courses or resources that belong in the track.
-   Open Learning Tracks from the sidebar or create a track from a content project.
-   Choose whether learners follow the track in a fixed or flexible order.

## Workflow

1. Open Learning Tracks and select a track. The Stages view shows the selected track and its ordered stages.
   ![Learning Tracks step 1](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks-step-1.png)
2. Open Track steps to review the steps attached to the selected track.
   ![Learning Tracks step 2](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks-step-2.png)
3. Use Create in Track steps to open the step form. Choose related content by its visible title, and cancel if you are only reviewing the available fields.
   ![Learning Tracks step 3](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks-step-3.png)
4. Select another track by its title to review that track's stages or steps without leaving the builder.
   ![Learning Tracks step 4](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks-step-4.png)
5. Open Reports to review activity for the selected track.
   ![Learning Tracks step 5](../.gitbook/assets/lms-user-guide/learning-tracks-step-5.png)

## Screen Details

| Area               | How to use it                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Track selection    | The selected track scopes the related Stages and Track steps shown in the builder. Use its visible title to confirm the context.      |
| Stages             | Review stage order and use the available actions to maintain the learning path.                                                       |
| Track steps        | Review or create the steps attached to the selected track. Relation controls should show readable titles rather than raw identifiers. |
| Player and Reports | Use Player to inspect the learner experience and Reports to review available track activity.                                          |

## Result

Track stages and steps are stored as workspace content. The builder exposes the selected track's related records, the learner Player, and available Reports in separate tabs.

## What To Check

Track step pickers should display titles and statuses, not relation IDs.
